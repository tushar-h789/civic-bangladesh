## Product

- **Name:** Civic Bangladesh
- **Bangla name:** সিভিক বাংলাদেশ
- **English tagline:** Better Citizens. Better Bangladesh.
- **Bangla tagline:** সচেতন নাগরিক, সুন্দর বাংলাদেশ।

## Positioning

A civic-education and government-service learning platform for Bangladesh. Civic Bangladesh helps citizens build everyday civic habits **and** prepare to use government services correctly. It is a learning and preparation platform — not the official government application portal.

## Primary Goals

These two goals are equally important.

### 1. Civic Education

Teach Bangladeshi citizens:

- civic sense
- road and traffic responsibility
- cleanliness
- environmental responsibility
- public space etiquette
- public transport behavior
- social responsibility
- digital citizenship
- community responsibility

The civic journey on the product remains: **Learn → Practice → Participate → Inspire → Impact**.

### 2. Government Service Learning

Help citizens understand how to use government services correctly.

Users should be able to discover:

- service information
- eligibility
- required documents
- government fees
- processing time
- application process
- common mistakes
- related learning course
- course completion
- assessment
- certificate

Service categories are data structures inspired by government service ecosystems — not invented official partnerships:

- বিদেশগামী নাগরিকদের সার্টিফিকেট সত্যায়ন
- সনদ, প্রত্যয়ন, অনাপত্তি ও অনুমতি
- নিবন্ধন ও লাইসেন্স
- শিক্ষা
- ভূমি ও ইজারা
- কৃষি ও সার
- অর্থ ও ব্যাংক
- ব্যক্তিগত আবেদন ও অনুমতি
- অন্যান্য

## Core Product Model

**Government Service → Learn the Process → Watch the Course → Complete Assessment → Earn Certificate → Apply Through Official Government Service Platform**

Civic Bangladesh prepares the citizen. The actual application happens on the official government service platform, unless a future integration is officially authorized.

## Course Philosophy

Courses should be practical, short, affordable, and service-specific. Each course teaches the actual preparation process.

Example: "বিদেশগামী শিক্ষার্থীদের SSC সার্টিফিকেট সত্যায়ন — Step-by-Step"

## Target Users

- Everyday citizens
- Students
- Young people
- Teachers
- Schools
- Universities
- Communities
- Organizations
- NGOs
- CSR programs
- Future government partners (only after official confirmation)

## Revenue Model

Essential civic awareness content remains free.

Revenue can come from:

- low-cost government-service preparation courses
- premium course bundles
- assessments
- certificates where officially supported
- school programs
- organization programs
- sponsored awareness campaigns
- future institutional learning packages

Courses should remain affordable and citizen-friendly. Premium features must add genuine value on top of the free civic-education tier, not gate core civic education.

## Frontend Stack

- TypeScript

- shadcn/ui
- Redux Toolkit

- Client-side API integration

## Trust Rules

- Never invent government information.
- Never fabricate government certification.
- Never claim an official partnership without confirmation.
- Never fabricate service fees or processing times.
- Keep official service information clearly sourced/attributed.
- For the actual application, use an official-government-service CTA such as **Apply on Official Portal**.
- Keep Civic Bangladesh visually independent from the official government portal unless official branding is provided.
- Treat service categories and demo statistics as sample/catalog data, not official government data or live records.

## Hard Constraints

- Do **not** use Next.js Server Actions.
- Do **not** implement backend functionality.
- Do **not** pretend Civic Bangladesh is the official government application platform.
- Do **not** invent official government partnerships, data, fees, processing times, or certificates.
- Essential civic education must remain free.
